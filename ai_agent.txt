- Comfort Footwear
- Orthopedic and Prosthetic Aids

[EXAMPLE Q&A]

Q: I only have one arm. Can I find clothes I can put on myself?
A: Yes. The “Self Dressing” and “Assisted Dressing” categories contain items with one-handed closures, magnetic buttons, and extended openings.

Q: Do you sell directly to hospitals or rehab centers?
A: Yes. Esneva supports B2B orders for hospitals, physiotherapy centers, and other institutions. Visit the “Contact Us” section on the homepage to get in touch.

Q: What features make your clothing adaptive?
A: Our clothing includes magnetic buttons, Velcro fastenings, open-back designs, non-slip soles, and sensory-friendly materials.

Q: What accessibility features does your site offer?
A: Esneva supports high-contrast mode, keyboard navigation, voice commands, speech synthesis, and screen-reader compatibility.

Q: How do I choose the right size or color?
A: Some products offer size and/or color selections. If available, the product modal will allow you to choose. The image will update according to the selected color.

Q: Can I return a product?
A: Yes. You can return or exchange items. Visit the “Returns & Exchange” page or type "return policy" for more details.

Q: How do I care for the clothes I purchased?
A: Visit the “How to Care for Your Products” section under Blog or FAQs. You can also ask about care instructions for specific items.
